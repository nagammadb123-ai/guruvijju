# Appu's Birthday Universe ❤️

Open `index.html` in Chrome to preview.

## Flow
1. Kannada kavana (appears line by line) → 2. Fireworks / light show → 3. "I ❤️ U" page → 4. Main website
   (hero, childhood, Same-birthday section, Modern Purohita, hand-in-hand photos, opening love letter, tap-a-heart messages, timeline, final surprise)

## Add your photos (folder `photos/`)
Any file name works, just match it in `script.js` (top section).
Still to add: childhood-1..4.jpg and purohita-1..3.jpg. Couple photos are already in.
Missing photos show a pink "add photo" placeholder, so nothing breaks.
Tip: resize photos to ~1000px wide so the page loads fast on phones.

## Add your videos (folder `videos/`)
Your 4 videos are already in (edit `VIDEOS` in `script.js` to add more). Keep each under ~20 MB (compress with HandBrake) so it plays on phones. Music pauses while a video plays.

## Add your songs (folder `music/`)
Put your Kannada and Hindi MP3s here and name them song1.mp3 ... song4.mp3
(or edit the `SONGS` list in `script.js`). Music starts when he taps the first button.

## Edit the words
Everything personal (kavana, letter, hearts, timeline, captions) is at the top of `script.js`.
Replace the timeline with your real dates, and the letter with your own words.

## Publish
Option 1 (easy, private-ish): upload the folder to Netlify Drop (app.netlify.com/drop) and share the link.
Option 2: GitHub Pages — note the site and photos become PUBLIC.
Or just open it on your phone/laptop and show him in person. 🥹
